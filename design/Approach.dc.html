Our approach

nodes. bonds. blocks.

Every engagement moves through the same three steps, shaped around your business: understand each part, connect them, then build the changes. You always know what’s happening, why, and what it will deliver.

01 · node.

Understand each part on its own terms.

We start with people as well as data. Short interviews show who owns each number and where the team thinks the problems are. A health check then scores every data source, so you know which numbers to trust.

  • People and ownership interviews
  • Data health check for every source
  • Customer voice: reviews, tickets and returns

02 · bond.

Connect what nobody has had time to connect.

The answers sit between the parts: margin joined to marketing, customers joined to products, what customers do joined to what they say. Then we bring the people who own those numbers together to agree what they mean.

  • Cohorts and lifetime value
  • Unit economics and true margin
  • Marketing efficiency, funnel and range analysis
03 · block.

Build changes, not decks.

Every finding leaves as a block: a change with an owner, a date, a KPI and a value in pounds. Changes backed by more evidence rank higher. Then we help your teams deliver them.

  • A ranked register of changes
  • Hands-on delivery across teams
  • A weekly trading pack to track every change

04 · Repeat

Then we do it again.

Every quarter we re-run the diagnostic, so you see progress in the same terms as the first look. Each change makes the next one easier. That’s the compound effect.
